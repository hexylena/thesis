\lettrine[lines=3,lraise=-0.1,lhang=0.3,findent=0.5em,nindent=0em]{\textcolor{hexy-red}{\fontEBGaramondInitials{}M}}{\color{hexy-red}oving from the cell to the clinic}, the focus is expanded to larger and more complicated organisms. Tumour necrosis factor (TNF) is a cytokine expressed mostly by immune cells, regulating inflammation, apoptosis, and cancer cell death. TNF-$\alpha$ affects the permeability of pericyte membranes making it an interesting target for drug delivery directly to the tumour microenvironment.

This chapter explores an RNA-Seq analysis of endothelial cells and pericytes when exposed to TNF, as well as the interactions between pericytes and endothelial cells, investigating the cellular responses with consideration to future potential drug delivery.
Additionally a system for securely obtaining sensitive human datasets in the clinic, such as RNA-Seq datasets, is investigated. Such research currently faces significant challenges due to concerns from data providers as well as hospital administrators over securing sensitive data. The proposed solution reduces barriers and makes that data more accessible without compromising on security.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\ref*{chapter:clinic}.\arabic*]
\itemsep-0.5em
\setcounter{enumi}{-1}
\item \textbf{\color{hexy-red}Transcriptome analysis reveals an important role for pericytes in TNF-induced inflammatory response.} We analysed the expression changes of endothelial cells and pericytes under the condition of Tumor Necrosis Factor (TNF), as well as the impact of TNF exposed pericyte medium upon endothelial cells. I completed the analysis of the RNA Sequencing data and associated visualisations, confirming the up-regulation of CXCL and CCL chemokines and inflammation associated pathways.

\item \textbf{\color{hexy-red}FAIR Data Retrieval for Sensitive Clinical Analysis in Galaxy.} Here we discuss improvements in the connection between Galaxy and EGA, the European Genome Archive, and the development of a wrapper that more easily integrates the two data stores. I contributed to the implementation of the Galaxy integration.


\end{enumerate}
