Most folks would love to be able to cast a magic spell, speak a secret word, and transform their pile of unanalysed data into a \emph{Nature} paper, ready to publish. If only this were possible! While a lot of modern computation---especially machine learning and large language models---may look like magic, it isn't. It's algorithms and statistics all the way down. However it can still feel like magic for those not learned in the Dark Arts of Electrified Silicon.

So what we can do is share the magic; in my time I've learned a lot of \emph{spells} that I can cast to make minor improvements to data, to clean and transform and even whole \emph{rituals} one can prepare to take data and turn it into a near-final product. This book collects some of those arcane arts in a reusable manner such that all bioinformaticians can \emph{cast} them on their own, without needing their local computer witch to help.
