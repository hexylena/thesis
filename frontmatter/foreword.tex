Most folks would love to be able to cast a magic spell, speak a secret word, and transform their pile of unanalysed data into a \emph{Nature} paper, ready to publish. If only this were possible! While a lot of modern computation---especially machine learning and large language models---may look like magic, it isn't. It's algorithms all the way down. Yet it can still feel like magic for those not experienced in the dark arts. What I can do is share the magic, do a bit of \emph{Open Sourcery}.

In my time I've developed a lot of \emph{spells} that I can cast to make minor improvements to data, to clean and transforming the results. Even whole \emph{rituals} one can conduct to transform raw data into (near-) final products. This book collects some of those arcane arts in a reusable manner such that all bioinformaticians can \emph{cast} them on their own, without having to make any deals with the devil, nor needing to call on their local computer witch for help.
