Most folks would love to be able to cast a spell and transform their pile of unanalysed data into a \emph{Nature} paper, ready to publish. And while a lot of modern computation, especially machine learning and large language models may look like magic, it isn't. It's statistics all the way down.

But what we \emph{can} do is share the magic; in my time I've learned a lot of ``spells'' that I can cast to make minor improvements to data, to clean and transform and even whole ``rituals'' one can prepare to take data and turn it into a near-final product. This book collects some of those spells and rituals in a reusable manner such that all bioinformaticians can ``cast'' them on their own, without needing their local computer witch to help.
