\chapter{Acknowledgments}
\vspace{-1cm}

Looking back, I'd say that when I started this PhD I wasn't really anything resembling functional. I lacked direction, I lacked Purpose, I was emotionally unstable, which---as we all know---is exactly the time to start a doctoral program! And now? Look at me now! Spot the difference!\footnote{Ah, somehow this turned into a self acknowledgement.\protect\footnotemark}
\footnotetext{Then again writing a thesis is inherently a somewhat self aggrandising act in the first place, a book plastered with your name to say I \emph{know} something, so acknowledging my own role is perhaps only appropriate, though I don't think my contribution is anything special.}

I do want to start by acknowledging the difficulty that is writing acknowledgements. I may have personally written a significant portion of the words in this thesis\footnote{considering co-authorship}, and every single word in the introduction and discussion myself; but that is clinical scientific language, something I excel at, comparatively. I've written these for each of you individually, thus their tone and length can vary.
